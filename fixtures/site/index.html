What we do

Northwind Robotics designs autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) and the fleet-management software that coordinates them across large distribution centers. Our platform helps warehouse operators move inventory faster while lowering operating costs.

Our mission

We believe physical logistics should be as programmable as software. Founded in 2018, we now operate fleets across 40 facilities in North America and Europe.
